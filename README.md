# Information Systems Audit Project using COBIT 2019

This repository contains an academic information systems audit project completed as part of the Control and IT Audit course at Universitas Mikroskil.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the IT governance condition of a case study organization, Perusahaan Inovasi Digital (PID), using the COBIT 2019 framework. PID is described as a fast-growing digital company that relies heavily on cloud-based platforms and information technology services.

The analysis focuses on identifying IT governance issues, assessing risk and compliance concerns, mapping COBIT 2019 design factors, selecting relevant governance and management objectives, and developing an audit design based on key COBIT domains.

## Case Study Issues

The main issues identified in the case study include:

- Lack of formal IT governance structure and documentation
- High dependency on third-party IT service providers
- Inadequate Service Level Agreement (SLA) management
- Absence of structured IT incident recording and monitoring
- Weak IT risk management and compliance practices
- Limited top management involvement in IT governance

## Framework Used

This project uses the **COBIT 2019** framework, especially the design factor approach, to map organizational conditions into relevant governance and management objectives.

The analysis includes several COBIT 2019 design factors, such as:

- Enterprise Strategy
- Enterprise Goals
- IT Risk Profile
- IT-Related Issues
- Threat Landscape
- Compliance Requirements
- Role of IT
- Sourcing Model for IT
- IT Implementation Methods
- Technology Adoption Strategy

## Key COBIT Domains

Based on the analysis, several COBIT domains were identified as relevant to the case study:

- **EDM** – Evaluate, Direct and Monitor
- **APO** – Align, Plan and Organise
- **BAI** – Build, Acquire and Implement
- **DSS** – Deliver, Service and Support
- **MEA** – Monitor, Evaluate and Assess

## Audit Focus Areas

The audit design focuses on five key COBIT 2019 processes:

- **EDM02 – Ensure Benefits Delivery**
- **APO10 – Manage Vendors**
- **APO12 – Manage Risk**
- **DSS02 – Manage Service Requests and Incidents**
- **MEA03 – Monitor, Evaluate and Assess Compliance**

These processes were selected to evaluate whether IT services deliver business value, vendor relationships are properly managed, IT risks are identified and mitigated, incidents are handled effectively, and compliance with external requirements is maintained.
